Players restore an earthquake-damaged town through farming, fishing, mining, crafting, ranching, magic, exploration, relationships, marriage, and seasonal events.

## 🔥 Project Overview

**This tool** is a comprehensive trainer for **Fields of Mistria**. A single-player trainer that provides runtime quality-of-life options for farming, exploration, resource gathering, inventory management, daily scheduling, and combat in Fields of Mistria.

**Target Audience:** Players who want to reduce repetitive resource gathering, extend farming and exploration sessions, simplify mine combat, experiment with progression, or customize the pace of a single-player playthrough.

> **⚠️ IMPORTANT:** This tool is for **educational and private use only**. It does not modify any game files — all changes are temporary and reset when the game closes. The tool is **undetectable**, but **use it at your own risk**. Always back up your save files before use.

## ⚡ Features (v1.0)

### 🎯 Gameplay

- **Unlimited Stamina** — Prevents stamina loss from activities such as watering crops, hoeing, mining, fishing, and other stamina-consuming actions.
- **One-Hit Tools** — Allows equipped tools to break rocks, trees, and other resource nodes with a single successful action.
- **Movement Speed** — Adjusts the player’s walking speed for faster travel across the farm, town, roads, ruins, and mines.
- **Easy Crafting** — Allows crafting and cooking stations to complete selected recipes without consuming the required materials.

### ⚔️ Combat

- **Unlimited Health** — Keeps health from decreasing when the player is hit by monsters in the mines.
- **One-Hit Combat** — Increases weapon damage so mine monsters can be defeated with a single successful strike.
- **No Fainting** — Prevents the player from fainting when health reaches zero during mine exploration and keeps the current day active.

### 🧰 Inventory

- **Unlimited Item Quantity** — Prevents selected stackable items from decreasing when used, sold, donated, crafted, cooked, or delivered for requests.

### 🌍 World

- **Freeze Daily Time** — Pauses the in-game clock while the option is active, allowing more time for farming, errands, fishing, and exploration.

### 📌 Farming

- **Instant Crop Growth** — Advances planted crops toward harvest without waiting through multiple in-game days.

## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

Launch the game first, load a save, and then activate the trainer. Toggle movement speed only when the player character is controllable. Return the value to normal before entering menus, cutscenes, festivals, or scripted events.

### Tools & Inventory

Open the inventory after loading a save and select the item or stack affected by the quantity option. Keep the feature disabled when buying, selling, donating, completing requests, or moving items between storage if you want the game to process normal quantity changes.

### World & Environment

Activate Freeze Daily Time after the day has started to pause the clock while completing farm work, visiting villagers, fishing, collecting materials, or exploring. Disable it before sleeping, advancing a festival, triggering a timed event, or changing areas if a transition does not respond normally.

### Combat & Weapons

Enter the mines and wait until the player can move and attack before enabling health or damage options. Unlimited Health prevents damage from mine enemies, while One-Hit Combat increases sword damage. Disable combat options before scripted encounters and save after leaving the mines.
